🎙️ Record your lines

Pick a line, hit Record (or Space), say it, hit Stop (or Space). It saves itself and jumps to the next line. ↑↓ move, P plays it back. Silence at the start and end gets trimmed.
Change the words with ✏️ (or E, or double-click the line). It saves into the game right away; refresh the game to see it.
